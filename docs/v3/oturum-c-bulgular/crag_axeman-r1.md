# crag_axeman — eleştiri r1: bulguları (verdict: fail)

Round-1 critique of crag_axeman. I filmed the sheet P1 (all anims, 3x and 4x) and these duel scenarios, mostly at 33–60 ms per frame and 2–5x zoom: caxSummon/P2, caxAttack/P2, the attack from zone 0 into zone 4 (staged through --eval), caxDirect, caxBlocked, caxLoser, caxHit, caxGuard, caxKill/P2. I also cut clip.mjs MP4s with sound for 8 scenarios. Evidence is in /home/user/wt/crag_axeman/shots/kartc/crag_axeman/r1c/ (atkz-*.png, atk-contact4x*.png, sumz-*.png, sumP2z*.png, killz-*.png, killz-5x.png, killP2z.png, blockz.png, block5x.png, loser.png, hitz.png, seam-pool.png, sheet/, sheet4/, clip/*.mp4|json).

**Fixes from the last round that did land:** typecheck is clean. The crackle run pans attacker→target: HF L/R goes from +5.8 dB to −7.5 dB over about 250 ms, and P2 is mirrored. The P2 sprite is relit and mirrored correctly. Peak is −1.8 to −2.0 dBFS with 0 clipped samples. The step sounds rise in level (−6, −3.5, −2.1 dBFS). No banned recipe names appear in the card's own files. The death split is the best moment: the axe bite, the seam climbing his centre line, the halves tilting apart and the seam-burn.

**What fails the bar (crystal_wyrm / magma_titan / coral_serpent):**
1. The signature attack never travels. The "three heavy accelerating steps" are stomps in place, because land ≈ home on the facing tiles. Zone 0 against zone 4 is also adjacent, so this is the normal case.
2. Two spin frames show the axe head floating with no arm or haft attached, over a flat burlap-box back view.
3. The war-paint pixels float in the gap between the halves during the split death. This is a visible bug in both P1 and P2.
4. When this card is struck or guards, the banned generic impactHeavy / impactLight play, because it has no fur/hide material.

Beyond those: the third step is merged with the chop sound, the summon has a pop-in and a door-like split, the nara picture is far shorter than its sound, the chop has a weak contact pose, the Yarık reads as a stick lying on the floor rather than a crack, and the head and axe read poorly at 1×.

- **[blocker] attack / travel (src/cinematics/kartc/crag_axeman.ts dagYaran geometry)** — The three heavy accelerating steps go nowhere: his feet stay on the home tile from the cock to the bite. The bite point is computed as front − 16 px, and land = bite − muzzleOffset. On the facing tiles (P1 z2 against P2 z2 at about (285,190)→(345,150), and z0 against z4) this gives land ≈ home, so P[0..4] are all about the same point. The run-up ('spin – stomp-stomp-STOMP – CHOP') reads as marching in place, and the bite lands about 25 px short of the target's feet, so the seam is tiny.
  - Düzeltme: Give the run-up real distance. Back-step 6–8 px during the spin/cock (so the travel starts behind home), and/or plant the bite just in front of the target's front edge (gap 4–6 px, not 16). That gives at least 20–24 px over the three steps, split about 9 / 7 / 6 px (long, then shorter and quicker, matching the 220/170/130 accent). Then take land from the contact frame's muzzle reach. Assert in the scenario test that |land − home| ≥ 18 px for the facing pair.
  - Kanıt: atkz-440.png / atkz-1240.png (1040–1920 ms: the body x is constant, only a foot lifts), atk-still.png at 1100 ms (cocked on his own tile), far-wide.png (z0→z4: same, no travel), atkP2-a.png (P2 the same).
