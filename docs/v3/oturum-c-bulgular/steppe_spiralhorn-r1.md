# steppe_spiralhorn — eleştiri r1: bulguları (verdict: fail)

Verdict: fail. I filmed steppe_spiralhorn at commit 60fd0f8 (clean tree) and looked at every PNG: sheets P1 and P2, close-ups at 3–6×, and 33–80 ms films of sshAttack, sshAttackP2, sshSummon, sshSummonP2, sshKill, sshKillP2, sshBlocked, sshDirect, sshHit, sshGuard and sshLoser. I also probed the sounds and made one clip of sshAttack with audio. Two problems break the card.

1. The attacker teleports to his home tile in the middle of his own strike, then pops back. I saw it in sshAttack, sshBlocked and sshLoser.
2. As player 2 the attack never reaches its target. The horn toss, flash and -900 land on empty floor about 40 px from the antelope.

Below those, five moments are clearly under the crystal_wyrm / abyss_magus bar:
- the third bound hangs with the horns already inside the target for about 230 ms before contact;
- the seed burst does not read;
- the summon's grass sits frozen and the gust cannot be seen;
- the death pops, stalls and ends on the shared white starburst and ring;
- the body is a flat rigid capsule.

The bounds also curve away from the target first.

What works:
- 3 bounds that rise in height, with grass fans at take-off and the flute rising per bound;
- the tiptoe landing, the rear and honk, and the direct attack;
- the sound is mostly solid: sounds land on their frames, peak is -2.0 dBFS with no clipping, the honk has nasal formants and the death flute glides D6 to A5.

One SFX ban is broken: shared `impactHeavy` plays under the horn crack.

The builder's claims are only partly true. "Every scenario filmed in P1 and P2 and fits budget" does not hold, because P2 is broken. The palette check failure on white is still unexplained.
