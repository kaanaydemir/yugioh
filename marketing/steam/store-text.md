# Prism Duel — Steam store text (draft v1)

## Short description (≤ 300 characters)
A hand-crafted pixel-art card duel where every card is a show. Summon living holograms onto a glowing
isometric arena, set traps, and watch each monster burst in, strike and shatter with its own animation and
sound. Build your own decks and duel a friend on one screen or the CPU.

## About this game (long description)

**Every card is a show.**
Prism Duel is a fast, tactical card duel with a hand-made pixel-art soul. Every monster has its own summon,
attack, guard and death — no two cards share a signature moment — and every effect has its own
synthesized sound.

**Summon. Set. Strike.**
- Normal and Tribute Summon monsters, set them face-down, flip them at the right moment.
- Spells and Traps change the flow: fields, equips, quick-plays and traps that fire in response to an attack.
- Short, punchy duels: 4000 LP, 40-card decks, every turn matters.

**80 cards at launch**
41 monsters, 28 spells and 11 traps — dragons of crystal and ash, a clockwork jester who flips a coin with
your fate, a magnetic sovereign assembled from three polar pieces, and five sealed fragments that win the
duel the moment they meet in your hand.

**A living arena**
A neon night stadium in crisp isometric pixel art: soft light pools, glowing tiles, drifting fog, torches and
holograms that react to the duel. Original pixel language at 640×360, scaled cleanly to any screen.

**Ways to play**
- 2 Players on the same screen (hot-seat, with a turn curtain so hands stay secret)
- Versus CPU
- Deck Builder: build, save and name your own 40–80 card decks (up to 3 copies of a card)
- Card Gallery: every card, every monster animated live
- How to Play: an animated tutorial of phases, summons, battle math and traps
