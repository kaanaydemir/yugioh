Quick start

Tap the title screen, pick VERSUS CPU (or 2 PLAYERS to pass the phone to a friend), choose a deck and duel. Bring your opponent's Life Points from 4000 to 0 to win.

FAQ

How do I build my own deck?
Main menu → DECK BUILDER. Tap a card to see it, use + / − or drag cards onto the deck and back out. Save to one of five slots.
Does the game need the internet?
No. Prism Duel runs fully offline and never connects to a server.
Where are my decks saved?
Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
Is there anything to buy inside the game?
No. Prism Duel is a one-time purchase: all cards and modes are included, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
