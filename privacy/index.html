PRISM DUEL

Privacy Policy · effective November 14, 2026

Short version

Prism Duel does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your decks, settings (sound, music, speed) and progress are saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. They never leave your device and are removed when you delete the app.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No network connections: the game runs fully offline.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
